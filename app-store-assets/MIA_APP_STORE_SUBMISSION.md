# Mia AI — Shopify App Store submission draft

## App name
Mia AI

## App introduction
AI-powered Shopify growth copilot for SEO, marketing, products, and store insights.

## Short description
Mia AI connects to a merchant's Shopify Admin and helps analyze products, improve SEO, generate ready-to-post marketing content, import products from CSV, and surface store intelligence.

## Core features
- Live Shopify product and inventory intelligence
- AI SEO packages with merchant-controlled Shopify write-back
- Product description, title, tags, handle, and image-alt optimization
- Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, paid-ad, and email campaign generation
- Shopify product and CSV import workflows
- Orders, customer and store analytics views using granted Shopify permissions
- Embedded Shopify Admin experience with session-token authentication

## Integrations
- OpenRouter AI
- PostgreSQL
- Cloudflare

## Review test flow
1. Install Mia from the Shopify-managed installation flow.
2. Open the embedded Mia AI app in Shopify Admin.
3. Confirm the store connects without manual shop-domain entry.
4. Open Mia and confirm live product/inventory context.
5. Select a product and generate an SEO package.
6. Review and apply SEO changes to the Shopify product.
7. Generate Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, paid-ad, and email content.
8. Open Product Import and preview/run a CSV import.
9. Verify the resulting Shopify product.
10. Uninstall and reinstall the app to verify re-authentication.
11. Verify webhook delivery and compliance behavior.
